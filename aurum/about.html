Heritage

Four decades, one thread

1987

Adriana Voss, a Biella-trained tailor and former heptathlete, cuts the first AURUM piece: a merino training layer sewn on a couture bench. She numbers it 001 and refuses to sell it.

1996

The house opens its Kyoto knitting atelier. The first recycled performance yarn enters the archive, decades before the industry follows.

2008

Zürich laboratory founded. StormShell 3L debuts on an Alpine expedition and returns without a failed seam. The lifetime seam warranty begins.

2019

The Customization Studio opens: every client may commission a one-of-one, drafted to their pattern, never repeated.

Today

Worn in 140 countries. Still numbered by hand. Still one bolt, one edition, one standard.

Client Care

The house stands behind every seam

Shipping & returns

Complimentary worldwide express, 2–4 days, duties included. Thirty days to return any uncustomized piece in exhibition condition; one-of-ones from the Studio are yours alone and cannot be returned, only repaired.

Care guide

Merino: cold hand wash, dry flat, never wring. StormShell: machine cold, reproof yearly. Every piece ships with its own care card, and the atelier answers care questions for life.

Store locator

Flagship maisons in Milan, Tokyo, Zürich, New York and Singapore. Private appointments through your concierge or the contact form below.

Privacy & terms

Your measurements, designs and acquisition history belong to you. The house keeps them encrypted, never sells them, and deletes them on request.
