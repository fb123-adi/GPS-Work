# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Affluent, design-literate shoppers of luxury sportswear: executives, athletes, collectors, and gift buyers who already shop Moncler, Loro Piana, and flagship Nike/LV collabs. They arrive on good hardware, often in the evening, browsing for pleasure as much as purchase. The job to be done is not "find a hoodie" — it is *feel that this house is worth belonging to*, then acquire a piece (or design a one-of-one in the studio).

## Product Purpose

AURUM is a global luxury sportswear house ("Luxury. Performance. Legacy."). This site is its digital maison: home, four collection floors (Men / Women / Unisex / Kids, 40+ pieces each), named luxury capsules, a product atelier view with a 3D viewer, a Customization Studio (real-time 3D garment configurator), and a tiered Membership salon. Success: a visitor's first impression is a fashion house, not a store; every interaction feels expensive; the studio makes them want to create.

## Brand Personality

Sovereign, machined, unhurried. The voice of a maître d' in a private showroom: few words, perfect certainty, no exclamation marks. Emotional goals: exclusivity, craftsmanship, calm confidence. Nothing appears instantly; everything arrives.

## Anti-references

- Generic dark-SaaS landing pages (gradient text, glass cards everywhere, hero-metric rows).
- Big-box sportswear e-commerce (dense promo grids, red SALE badges, urgency banners, countdown timers).
- Editorial-magazine affectation (italic display serif + mono labels + ruled columns) — this is a maison, not a magazine.
- Anything that reads "template": identical card grids, eyebrow kickers over every section, emoji as icons.

## Design Principles

1. **Museum, not marketplace.** Products are exhibited one idea per fold — pedestals, spotlights, provenance copy — never crammed.
2. **Gold is a signature, not a paint bucket.** Near-black drench carries the house; gold appears where a hand would touch (CTAs, hairlines, the mark).
3. **Nothing appears instantly.** Motion is choreographed arrival — but content is always visible without it, and reduced-motion is a first-class experience.
4. **Depth is real.** 3D, light, and parallax are load-bearing craft (WebGL scenes, true perspective), not decorative blur.
5. **Performance is part of luxury.** 60fps, lazy assets, no jank; a stutter is a loose thread on a bespoke suit.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA: body text ≥4.5:1 on the near-black surfaces, visible focus rings (gold, 2px), full keyboard paths for nav/cart/configurator, `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for every animation, semantic landmarks and labels, touch targets ≥44px. 3D scenes are progressive enhancement with static fallbacks and descriptive text.
