# Product

## Register

brand (home, collections, about, journal); product (shop grid, product page purchase panel, cart, checkout, account, admin).

## Users

Urban Indian professionals and serious amateurs, roughly 25 to 45, who train three to five times a week and dress simply the rest of the time. They already buy from Lululemon, Uniqlo U, Nike, and a few Indian D2C labels, and they are tired of loud sportswear marketing. Most sessions are on a phone in the evening; many return on a laptop to buy. They want to know the fabric, the fit, and the delivery date, then pay by UPI in under a minute.

## Product Purpose

Kyveron sells premium daily wear and training garments, made in India, direct to customers. The site has to make a first-time visitor trust an unfamiliar Indian brand enough to pay full price: clear fabric facts, honest stock, visible return terms, and a checkout that never makes them wonder whether their money went through. Success: product photography dominates, the purchase path is short, and nothing feels like a template.

## Brand Personality

Measured, tailored, quiet. The voice of a good tailor who also coaches: short sentences, exact numbers (220 GSM, 5 in inseam), no hype, no exclamation marks, no invented heritage. Performance is shown through specifics, not adjectives.

## Anti-references

- Big-box sportswear e-commerce: promo banners, red SALE badges, countdown timers, fake "only 2 left" urgency.
- Fake luxury: gold foil, serif monograms, words like "exquisite" or "curated", invented heritage dates.
- Dark SaaS landing pages: gradient text, glass cards, neon accents, hero-metric rows.
- Editorial-magazine affectation: italic display serif, mono labels, ruled columns.
- Gradients anywhere.

## Design Principles

1. **The garment is the hero.** Photography gets the space; UI chrome recedes.
2. **Facts over adjectives.** Fibre, weight, fit, and care are always one glance away, set with the same care as the headline.
3. **Cobalt means performance or action.** It appears on links, selected states, and performance details, never as decoration.
4. **Calm motion.** One motion language: short, eased, transform and opacity. Nothing blocks shopping, checkout, or payment.
5. **Honest commerce.** Real stock, real delivery windows, prices from the server, clear taxes and returns.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Body text at least 4.5:1, focus rings visible on every surface, full keyboard paths through the cart drawer, filters, and checkout, touch targets at least 44px, reduced motion honoured live, alt text on every product image, forms with labels and specific error messages. Prices use Indian number formatting by default.
